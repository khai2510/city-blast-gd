## What is good (keep)
- **Art and level identity.** The toy city, the heroes (windmill, lighthouse, clock tower, arch, bridge boss, Sky Saucer) and the Home screen all read as a store-quality game. All three testers said so (u01, g07–g09).
- **The new L1 double-tap teach works:** dim, gold spotlight, "Double-tap!" and the hand. A slow double tap still blasts (u02).
- **The L2 BLAST card, the material cards (Brick/Concrete/Steel) and the L6 swipe hand** are clear, one line each with one button (u05, x05, u13).
- **The sawtooth is labelled:** first Hard on L7 (purple), boss on L10 (orange), relief after each. Rewards scale 10/20/30.
- **Unlimited waves make failing forgiving.** A bad first wave on L3 was still recoverable to 88%.
- **The Settings sheet:** 48 px rows, destructive reset demoted and confirmed.

## 3 recommended fixes for the Lead

1. **[A] Fix the L4 Concrete teach within the decided rules.**
   - Aim the spotlight at a visible grey column and let the spotlight tap place a TNT there, as on L1.
   - Open L4 at a yaw where at least 2 columns are pickable.
   - Change the fail tip to taught words ("Hit the grey columns.").
   - Re-gate L4 naive with Blast Power 0 and 1 (target: naive ≤ 70%).
   - Also align `INTRO_SCHED` with D-blast-SCHED: boosters L12/22/33, one new thing on L11, no L17 rc card.

   Covers GD-1, GD-2, GD-3 and UX-10. The hero's shape is not touched (HERO-PROC).
2. **[A] Polish the HUD and fail screen:**
   - Pause button inside the top safe area (UX-1).
   - Readable "85%" at the tick, ≥ 16 px (UX-3).
   - Hide the rescue button when it is unaffordable, so Try Again is the only primary (UX-6).
   - Do not show debris coins that are later taken back (UX-13).
   - Fix the Map title overlap (UX-8).

   These are bugs and polish under existing decisions.
3. **[B — needs Owner]** Two decisions:
   - **Win moment:** (a) hold 1.2 s on the wreck with the existing "DEMOLISHED!" title and a coin burst before going Home, or (b) show the existing result card on every win (UX-2/GD-6).
   - **Pre-unlock clutter:** hide the Shop tab until the store exists, hide the locked booster tray and the all-locked pre-level picker until L10 is beaten (UX-4/5/7, GD-8).

   Optional third topic: re-price the economy (Coin Bonus, total cost against coins earned in 36 levels; GD-7).
