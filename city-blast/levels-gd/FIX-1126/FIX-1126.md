## 1. UX-105: a tap off the building left the reticle red under "Tap to swing" for good

**Cause.** `bstTap`'s no-member path (sky, grass, or a protected building: `pick` has no member there) calls
`ballRet(true, x, y, bad)` and `toast("Tap the building")` but counts no refusal, so FX25 W1 (which restores after a *counted*
refusal) never put the ring back.
**Fix (FX26 R1).** The same path as FX25 W1: the reason goes in the aim tip under the red ring, and 1.4 s later the white ring and
"Tap to swing" come back on the spot a swing accepts (`ballRet(true)` + `ballSuggest()`). When the tap ray hits a protected
building's box, the reason is the Ball's own existing line "Protected! Swing at the target building."; otherwise the game's
"Tap the building". The loose toast of that tap is cleared (the tip already says it).

| Level 48, seeded save, Ball armed | Before (`ceafd0a`) | After |
|---|---|---|
| tap the sky (196, 150), 0.3 s | red ring, tip "Tap to swing / Tap the chip to cancel", loose toast "Tap the building" | red ring, tip "Tap the building", no loose toast |
| same, 2.2 s | **still red**, "Tap to swing" | white ring, "Tap to swing / Tap the chip to cancel" |
| tap the protected building (328, 519), 0.3 s / 2.2 s | red + "Tap to swing" / still red | red + "Protected! Swing at the target building." / white + "Tap to swing" |
| tap (279, 514) (FX25 refused path) | reason, white after 1.4 s | unchanged |

## 4. U-2: the refill button looked pressable when coins are short

**Cause.** `bstBuyOpen` already sets `#bbBuy.disabled` when coins < price, but uiclean B1 forces the green "go" gradient on
`.bov .bcard .big.mint` with `!important`, so the disabled button stayed bright green.
**Fix (FX26 R4), CSS only.** A disabled `.bov .bcard .big.mint` takes the game's own disabled-button look (the `--off` tokens of
`.big:disabled`), with dark text so the price stays readable, and no press motion. Prices, pack and the text are unchanged.

## 5. U-4: does the Safety Net do anything?

**Finding: it works; it was mute.**
- Level 38 (Telephone Exchange, a blue building on the right), seeded save, real tap on the Net chip: the chip gets its "on" ring,
  stock stays 2. On the real BLAST tap `wreckLead` → `netBuild` builds **1 fixed fence** (`__blast.boost.netUp()` = 1) and spends
  the use (stock 2 → 1). The plan is a 3.0 m high, 0.3 m thick fence at x 4.22–4.52, z −2.81…2.81, in the gap between the target
  and the blue building (x ≥ 6.36). The level has no street, so there is no kerb fence.
- The fence is a real Rapier body: the same solver plan ends wave 1 at 70.7 % without the net and 67.9 % with it (rubble lands on
  the fence instead of rolling on). Collateral was 0 % in both, because that plan never sends rubble at the blue building. With a
  bad plan that topples the tower onto it, both runs fail on collateral (19 % without, 22 % with): the net catches rubble, not a
  falling building (the documented design, `docs/BOOSTERS.md` §4.2).
- **Why the player saw nothing.** (1) The arm line "Safety Net up on BLAST · catches rubble, not buildings" (and "Safety Net off",
  "Nothing to protect on this level", "No room for a net here") is a `toast()`, and uiclean mutes `toast()` in a level: the toast
  stayed at opacity 0. (2) The neighbour fence runs front-to-back between the two buildings, so from the planning camera it is seen
  nearly edge-on (0.3 m), under the dust.
- **Fix (FX26 R5).** On a Net chip tap the line the game wrote shows in the existing toast (written to `#toast` directly, as FX25
  and sflay do), 2.6 s. That line is longer than one row at 375–393 px (r19 keeps toasts on one line and it ran off the right edge),
  so this one toast may wrap inside the 16 px sides. No new text, element or mechanic. Making the fence itself easier to see would
  be a look change: not done (proposal below).

Shot: `f5-U4-net-armed-line.jpg` (before: no words; after: the line).
