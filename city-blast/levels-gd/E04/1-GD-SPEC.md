# E04 "Market Hall": step 1, GD spec (easy pack, Normal, TEACH)

**Slot 9 in the plan. Role: TEACH** (one new idea in a safe scene; failures are cheap and their cause is visible).
**Lesson (one line): wood falls easily, brick is a shield.** Read the materials: a bomb on brick takes one pier; a bomb on timber takes a whole section. The brick arcade stands between the street and the timber posts: open the shield first, then hit the wood.

**Inspiration:** the covered timber market halls of a market town (open timber posts, a clerestory, a big tiled roof with a little lantern) behind a brick street arcade, with a weigh house at each end. Inspiration only: our own three-section plan, our own sizes and colours, no real market. It does not duplicate any F-landmark, H1–H5 or E01–E06.

## Realization moment (no text, no arrows)
1. A bomb on the arcade: one pier and its arch drop out, the rest stands (a small step).
2. A bomb on a weigh house: a corner goes, the house stands.
3. Through the gap, or at the sides, a bomb on the timber: a whole section of the market folds and its tiled roof comes down whole; one more bomb cracks it. "Wood is where the gain is; brick is just in the way."

## Wow (chain)
Open the arcade, then one bomb on the timber at a section joint: two sections fold together, the tiled roofs slide into the street and push the broken arcade over.
