# E05 "Star Dome": step 1, GD spec (easy pack, Normal, TEACH)

**Slot 5 in the plan. Role: TEACH** (order of work).
**Lesson (one line): break the shell before the core.** E01–E04 taught where to hit (legs, two legs side by side, drums, timber behind brick). E05 adds *in which order*: a hard core you cannot reach until the soft shell around it is open.

**Inspiration:** a small public observatory: an eight-sided brick drum, a metal dome with an observing slit, a telescope on a massive pier that stands free of the building (so the floors' shaking never reaches the telescope). Inspiration only: our own plan, sizes and colours, no real works. It does not duplicate any F-landmark (no saucer, no dome hall, no lighthouse), H1–H5 or E01–E04/E06. H5 "Glass Spine" also has a core, but there the core is peeled over three blasts (`layer7`); here it is one ordinary hard piece per storey, hidden.

## Shape (x: left → right; z+: towards the camera)

| Building | Position | Size | Structure | Role |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| **Telescope pier** (A) | in the middle, through both floor holes | 2.0 m then 1.7 m square, 8 m tall | **two `hard7v` pieces** that touch nothing but each other and the ground: counted until a bomb cracks them, never brought down by the shell's fall. The telescope (white tube, mount, counterweight) is decor on the top piece, aimed out of the slit | **the core** |
| **Study wings** (B, C) | x ±10.6, 2.6 m gap | 6 × 7.2 m, 2 timber storeys + slate roof ≈ 9.5 m | timber posts (`wpost`), board walls (`wwall`) with arched windows, a glazed front, a light roof body (`g7roofL`) with a transit hut and a weather mast; desks, books and lamps inside (`floorIn`) | **easy**: one or two bombs each |

Ordered layout: one axis, the two wings the same size, the same gap, fronts in line; camera near frontal (yaw 0.10).

## Mechanics (all existing)
`hard7` (the pier counts until cracked), `drop7` on `mdB` (plumbing only: a floor up high ignores TNT, a floor in the heap cracks), separate bond groups (drum + dome + pier `bg 101`, wings `105`, `106`).

## Realization moment (no text, no arrows)
1. First tries go on the drum, because nothing else is there to tap: the drum and the dome fold, the wings go easily, and the bar stops short: the grey pier stands in the middle of the heap with the telescope still on it.
2. "The pier was inside all along." One bomb on each piece and it is done. The core is not tappable until the shell is open: from all four views the walls and the dome hide it.

## Wow (chain)
Bombs on the drum's posts: the dome sags into the drum, the drum's upper storey goes with it, and the brick shell falls away round the pier like a peeled fruit, leaving the telescope pointing at the sky.

## Tester targets (Normal, easy pack)
| Test | Bar |
|---|---|
| oneBomb | < 70 % |
| allFoot / footOnly | < 80 % |
| oneWave | reported only |
| fair | 3/3 wins, spare ≥ 2, ≥ 2 blasts, 20–75 s |
| lesson probe | shell only (every post of the drum, the pier untouched): % reported, must stay < 85 %; the pier must not be tappable from any of the 4 views while the drum stands |
| curve | no jump > 40 points between consecutive bomb counts |
| at rest | nothing moves or dies in 8 s; 0 bonds cracked |

Fail line (≤ 9 words, a principle): **"Open the shell first. The core still counts."** No opening tip.
