## 2. UX-88 + UX-91 + U-1 opening order

- `src/main/frame.js` F10: `__blast.frame.opening()` is true while the opening shot still moves:
  - the zoom-in, or the F7 front → 45° turn;
  - or the engine's yaw more than 1° behind it.
  It is false once a touch stopped the turn, after a blast, off the game screen, and 8 s after the level start at the latest. `holdSway(on)` holds the ±0.06 rad idle sway.
- `src/main/intro.js`: the level's cards, spotlights and the swipe lesson wait for `phase === "plan"` and `!opening()`.
- `src/main/tap2.js`: the L1 "Double-tap!" dim, ring, hand and label (and the L2 hand) wait for `!opening()` and for no pending intro card (`__blast.intro.busy()`). While the L1 prompt is up the idle sway is held, so the ring does not drift.

**L1 double tap ("2 of 3 starts placed 2 TNT, no BLAST"): it is the low-fps harness, not a game bug.**
- The tester's two CDP touches 140 ms apart are each held until the renderer acks them. At 6–12 fps the second tap's `timeStamp` comes 730–860 ms after the first, which is over the lesson's 600 ms. The game logged `tap:ok`, then `stack:ok:734` (a 2nd TNT): a slow tap.
- The same CDP double tap with real event times 140 ms apart fires BLAST in 6 of 6 starts:
  - 3 on the old build (`tap:ok`, `dbl:first:1`, `tutDone:t2l1`, phase `boom`);
  - 3 on the new build, on the still target.
- On a phone the event times are the hardware's. The remaining risk is a real slow double tap (> 600 ms) on L1. It is handled as before: a 3rd tap on the TNT blasts (`tutSlowDbl`). Not changed: changing it would be a rule change.
- SF4 (L1 lesson by real taps) passes 3 of 3.
