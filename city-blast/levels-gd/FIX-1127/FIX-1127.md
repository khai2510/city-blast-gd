## 1. GD-104 (P0): the Safety Net built its fence inside a building

**Cause.** `netPlan` puts the neighbour fence (3 m high, 0.3 m thick, a fixed Rapier body) at the midpoint between the blue building's
inner x edge and the target's x extent. Its only guard is `gap < minGap`; there is no overlap check. When the blue building stands
behind the target inside its x range, the midpoint lands inside a building. Read from the build, base plans (fence x · building it is in):

|---|---|---|---|
| 7 Grand Arch | two fences −6.37…−6.07, 7.63…7.93 | target (`cpan`, `concrete`) | both left out → no fence: the Net cannot be armed |

**Fix (FX27 N1).** A fence (street or neighbour) stays in the plan only when its box (0 … fence top) is clear of every live member's

says the game's own "Nothing to protect on this level" (or "No room for a net here" when a side is also too tight) and nothing is
armed or spent; the pre-level pick is not armed either (it reads the same plan). Spend rule unchanged (1 use when ≥ 1 fence goes up).

## 2. GD-109: heavy-piece levels 50 / 54 / 56 end in flat rubble; 54 never shows a result

**Cause (measured on 54 Grain Elevator, `?lv=54`, blind plan).**
1. **Taps do place on the rubble.** With fresh member positions read after the post-wave camera has settled (2.5 s), all 26 TNT went
   on (lying `rc` / `mdC` / `cpan` pieces included) and the level ended on its own: 3 waves, **80.9 %, "NOT ENOUGH"**, result card.
   The designer's tool (a) only tries *standing* members in wave 2 (`m.standing || W >= 2` with W = 1: why 50's "wave 2 placed
   nothing") and (b) taps screen positions read before the camera finished easing after the blast: the taps landed on nothing
   ("Tap a building"). Two of three tool runs reproduced the stall that way; the settled run did not. Not a Mac block / data cause.
2. **The soft-lock is real in the game rules:** `waveEnd` ends a level only on a verdict or when the stock is 0. With TNT left and no
   member a tap can place on (all down and gone, under the stage, out of reach from every view), nothing ends the level: the player
   sits in planning with TNT forever (GD-77).

**Fix (FX27 C1).** Once a second in planning (wave ≥ 2, TNT left, nothing placed, no booster armed or live, not in the test harness):
count the members a tap could place on from each of the four quarter views at the farthest pinch-out zoom (same `pick()` as a tap;
the stage / steel rack / protected buildings do not count). When none, twice in a row, the level ends with `finish()`, i.e. its normal
result card and verdict (the same as an end on stock 0). While anything is reachable nothing changes.

| 54, real taps wave 1 + BLAST | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| wave 2, members reachable (`fx27hosts()` ok, `hrc`) | stays in plan | stays in plan (no early end, 5 s) |
| wave 2, nothing reachable (forced by `fx27force()`) | stays in plan forever | result card in **3.9 s**: "NOT YET! 20 % Cracked concrete stays cracked. Hit it again. Try Again" |

The "Heavy pieces still count. Hit them." line stays true: lying heavy pieces are tappable. 50's tier label and 56's 97 → 73 % change
(one run each) are data / plan questions: asked in `SYNC.md`. Shot: `f2-GD109-54-end.jpg`.

## 3. GD-106: the Ball ring drawn white on a refused side

**Cause.** With no prompt spot on the side (`ballSug` null: the side's hazard, steel, nothing reachable) `ballSuggest` writes the
side's reason in the tip but `ballRet(true)` draws the ring white at the default spot; a tap there runs `ballThrow`'s own checks,
which do not include the side hazard. So on 41 / 52 the white ring refused ("Blocked: the school is in the way", "Can't reach that
spot from here") and on 55 the tip said "Street side · swipe" while a tap on the ring threw.

**Fix (FX27 B1).** While `ballSug` is null, the ring is read with `ballThrow`'s checks at its own spot (member under it, protected,
too close, can't reach, blocked) after every `ballSuggest` and again as the camera settles (0.45 / 1.1 / 2 / 3.2 s): refused → the
ring's existing red look with the side's reason in the tip; a swing would happen → white ring and "Tap to swing". Ball rules,
strength and reach are unchanged (a street-side swing on 55 still swings, as before; it now says so).

| Real taps, armed Ball, first side then swipes | Before: ring / tip → tap | After: ring / tip → tap |
|---|---|---|
| 41 side 0 | white / "School side · swipe" → refused "Blocked: the school is in the way" | **red** / "School side · swipe" → refused (same line) |
| 41 side 1 | white / "Tap to swing" → swings | white / "Tap to swing" → swings |
| 52 side 0, 1 | — | **red** / "Street side · swipe", "School side · swipe" → refused "Can't reach that spot from here" |
| 52 side 2 | — | white / "Tap to swing" → swings |
| 55 side 0 | white / **"Street side · swipe" → swings** (18 % in the tester run) | white / **"Tap to swing"** → swings |
| 51 side 0 | — | white / "Tap to swing" → swings |
