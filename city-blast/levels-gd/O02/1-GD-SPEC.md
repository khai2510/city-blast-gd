# O02 "Hop Kiln": step 1, GD spec (old polish, play slot 2, tutorial)

**Replaces:** old L2 "Stripey Point" (F28 lighthouse on legs and a row of timber boathouses), slot 2 of ORDER22.
**Intro role (kept):** the **BLAST** card ("Tap to place. BLAST to blow them all.") with the L2 hand (main/tap2.js H2: the hand taps
the two front piers of `sol[0]`'s face, then BLAST), then the **Brick** card ("Brick: one TNT per pier.") whose spotlight puts its
TNT on a brick member of the plan. Nothing else is new: wood + brick only.
**Lesson (one line): two piers side by side bring it down.** One pier alone only cracks it.

**Inspiration:** the brick hop kilns (oasts) of farm country: a square brick kiln with a tall tiled pyramid roof and a white timber
cowl, the cart lodge beside it, the pickers' cottages. Inspiration only: our own square kiln on a cart arcade, our sizes and colours.
Not an F-landmark (no lighthouse, windmill, clock tower), L01–L05 or E01–E10 (the E02 shot tower is a tall shaft on legs; this is a
squat kiln with a pyramid and cowl).

## Realization (no text)
The first try is often one pier: it cracks and the kiln leans but stays. The hand showed two side by side: the pair brings it down.

## Wow (chain)
Two piers and BLAST: the kiln pitches forward, the cowl and the pyramid crash through the cart lodge, the hop pockets spill.
