# SLOTS-50: blueprint for levels 1–50 (plan, 23/11, Agents-Game)

Khai 23/11, **D-blast-L50** + addendum: focus on 1–50 by the launch schedule (**D-blast-LAUNCH**). Hard-quality levels keep Hard slots
and are **placed as-is**, even with later elements (launch exception, hard levels only; their materials are decided later; no request to
the Mac). Every other level follows the schedule: a later element is converted to an earlier one. Plan only: no level, PLACE or card has changed yet.

Launch (teach) slots: wood 1 · brick 4 · concrete 8 · Wrecking Ball 12 · blue 15 · glass 20 · red barrels 25 · Net 30 · steps (multi-blast) 35 ·
steel 40 · Sky Strike 45 · heavy pieces 50 · later: dark RC 60 · drop 70 · trap 80 · stone 90 · per-blast 100.

## 0. Reading rules used (assumptions, Lead/Khai correct)

- **Element keys** (order-audit + OLD-POLISH): the audit's "dark" is split. **Heavy** = `hard7v / hard7t / hard7p` (launch 50).
  **Dark RC** = `rc / rcs / hrc` (launch 60). **Drop** = `drop7`, also when it is only "plumbing" on floors (E01, E10…), launch 70.
  **Steps** = the expert plan needs ≥ 2 blasts, launch 35. Before 35, the expert plan must win in **one blast** (as OLD-POLISH did before its slot 14).
- **Rotate** (swipe to turn) is not on Khai's list. It is treated as a control taught by the swipe hand. Its card moves to **16** (O05,
  the first turn level with a blue to avoid). Before 16, no non-hard level needs a turn.
- **Boss at 24 and 49, not 25 and 50.** 25 (barrels) and 50 (heavy) are launch slots, and a teach is never on a boss (game-designer §2). So
  the boss moves one slot earlier, and the launch becomes the relief right after it, which is the existing booster-card pattern. **Option for
  Khai:** keep the bosses on 25/50 and move the barrel and heavy launches to 26/51 (this changes D-blast-LAUNCH).
- **Sawtooth:** a Hard every 4–5 slots (7, 11, 14, 19, 29, 34, 39, 44) plus 2 bosses. Each one is followed by a **relief that is also the launch
  slot** (8, 12, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35, 40, 45, 50). That gives 10 hard-feeling slots out of 50 (20%). No two are in a row.
- **Converted levels are re-gated** on the bar they were built for (easy-pack / OLD-POLISH; slots 1–3 on tutorial bars, 5 TNT, K2).
  A conversion changes members and materials only, never the hero shape or lesson role, except where the lesson column says so.
- Old levels that were never rebuilt (old art, rubric 2.9) are rebuilt to the easy-pack bar when they are placed (Khai: "fix all easy levels not yet fixed").
