## 1. UX-98: a tap during the opening froze the camera turn

**Cause (`src/main/frame.js`).** The canvas `pointerdown` handler (F2 / F7 / F9) ended the zoom-in "where it is" (`zoomT = zoomK`) and
set the front → 45° turn to done at the yaw the camera had at that instant (`S.yawD = yaw − baseYaw`). Nothing ever resumed it, and a
later re-fit keeps that `yawD`, so one early tap fixed a between-poses view for the whole level.

**Fix (F11, `?ff=0` = old).** A touch now ends the zoom-in at its END zoom (`S.zOpen`) and plays the rest of the turn on the same ease
curve in 0.2 s (`S.ffK`). The tap that cuts the shot short is not used (it was aimed at a view that is about to turn, the F9 double-tap
miss): `frame.eatTap()` is true for a tap whose `pointerdown` came while the zoom-in or the turn still ran, in planning;
`src/main/polish.js` FX25 O1 drops it at the top of `onTap`. Later taps (also during the short yaw lag) place as usual (a first
version also dropped those; check-r22 B7 then lost its quick tap, so the window was narrowed). Taps during `build` are
untouched (hard levels: R22 queues and replays them on their member), so are intro / replay calls (no canvas `pointerdown`).

## 2. Wrecking Ball: "nothing swings" (U-5, UX Bug 1)

Two real code causes, both silent (not the low fps):
1. **A refused swing hid the aim prompt for good.** `bstTap` clears the prompt (`ballSug`, the "Tap to swing" tip) *before*
   `ballThrow` checks the spot. When the check refuses (spot hidden from the ball's side, too close to a blue building, blocked,
   protected) the reticle turns red on the tapped spot and the reason goes to `toast()`, which uiclean mutes in a level. Nothing put
   the prompt back, so the red ring stayed on the refused spot: a tap on it is refused again, and the Ball never swings (L12, first
   pickable member = a window wall behind the kiosk: `refused 1 → 2`, 0 throws, for good).
2. **After a swing every tap was swallowed in silence until the building settled** (`ballLive` holds up to `settleMax` 6 s game time
   after the hit, the ball winched out of view for most of it). At 8 fps that was 27 s wall time on L12 (U-5's "18 taps in 25 s").

**Fix (`src/main/polish.js` FX25 W1, `?fx25=0` = off).** A refused swing shows the game's own reason in the aim tip under the red ring
("Can't reach that spot from here", "Too close to the …", "Blocked: …"); 1.4 s later the ring and "Tap to swing" come back on the spot
a swing accepts (`ballSuggest`). A tap while the swing / settle holds says so in the existing toast: "Swinging…", then "Wait until it
stops moving". The wait itself, the swing, its checks and the physics are unchanged. The tip text "Tap to swing" is right (a tap on
the ring swings), so it stays.

|---|---|---|
| tap the first pickable member (wwall 181, 385) | red ring, no words, tip gone | red ring + "Can't reach that spot from here" |

| tap the ring | refused again (0 throws, never swings) | swings (throws 1, 12.8 % on the hit) |
| 8 taps during the swing / settle | (U-5: nothing) | "Swinging…" ×1, "Wait until it stops moving" ×7; then the ball is put away, taps place TNT |

## 3. UX-99: aim tip and ✕ off the screen edge

**Cause.** The tip is a child of the reticle, centred under it (`translateX(-50%)`) with no clamp; the sflay ✕ sits at
`--sfSide − 14 px` (= 0) and the R22 fallback at `max(4, …)`.
**Fix.** FX25 W2 shifts the tip so it stays 16 px inside the screen sides (the reticle's ×1.08 pulse included); `src/main/sflay.js`
puts the ✕ at `max(16px, --sfSide − 14px)` on both sides; the R22 fallback clamps to 16 px.
